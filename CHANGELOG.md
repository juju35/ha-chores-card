# Changelog

## 1.0.0 - 2026-11-22

### Features
- First release: `chores-card` and `chores-mini-card`. The day's chores of each person in one checklist, ticked off with a tap, the overdue ones first, a filter per person, and the kids' points and the reward in reach; an illustrated header; the mini card with a progress ring. Todo lists (Donetick and any `todo` entity), Chore Helper, Grocy chores, and points sensors (KidsChores, ChoreOps, `input_number`).
